About & Scientific Precision

This extension focuses on the highest standard of precision for French mathematics, artificial intelligence, computer science, and physics terminology. It distinguishes crucial scientific nuances such as corps (field vs. body), variété (manifold vs. variety), adhérence (closure vs. adherence), and noyau (kernel vs. nucleus).
